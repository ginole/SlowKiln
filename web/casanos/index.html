The story

Johnny & Elena's place.

Johnny and Elena Casano opened on Anne Street in 1992. Johnny passed in 2014; Elena kept the kitchen going, the way he would have wanted.

The rule on the wall hasn't changed in thirty years: be nice or leave. Mangia che ti passa — eat, and it'll pass.
