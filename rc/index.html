Vito's Pizza & Restaurant保留
Moncton, New Brunswick · GoHighLevel 订阅站 · 每页 1.2MB

独立单店，与俄亥俄那个带会员系统的连锁 vitos.com 无关。在给 agency 交月费，换来 2 页电话菜单，全站无一张食物照片；FB 上有 8 千粉丝和像样的披萨图，官网完全没用上。

邮件可用痛点（英文直接抄）
  • You pay an agency every month, and the whole site is two pages of menu text
  • There is not a single photo of your food anywhere on the site — for a pizza place
  • Your homepage is 1.2 MB of code before a single image loads — slow on every phone
  • The browser tab title just says “Takeout Menu” — not your name
  • Facebook says “since 1969”; the website never mentions it — your best selling point is missing

Mrs. White's Golden Rule Cafe保留
Phoenix, Arizona · Wix · 首页 855KB

地标级老店（1964 年、上过 Food Network、市府褒奖），网站明显没人管：社交图标链到 Wix 占位账号、catering 页全是库存占位图、站内两处价格对不上。站上有整组专业菜品摄影可直接用。

邮件可用痛点（英文直接抄）
  • The social icons on your site link to Wix’s placeholder accounts, not your pages
  • Your catering page shows stock placeholder images and a $0.00 minimum order
  • The site shows two different prices for the same meal ($18 on the chalkboard photo, $23 in the text)
  • Your homepage is 855 KB of code — heavy and slow on phones
  • You’ve been on Food Network — and the website never mentions it

Abe's Grill保留
Corinth, Mississippi · 无网站（FB 有登录墙）

TripAdvisor Corinth 第 1（52 家里），但线上资产为零。⚠️ 实地核验（他看了 Google Maps）：就是一间路边小屋 + 17 座柜台，客单 $10-20，店内陈设很旧 —— 付费能力弱。保留（冷邮件多发一家边际成本为零 + 50 年老店可能想留点念想），但放最低优先级，当 legacy sale 碰运气。

邮件可用痛点（英文直接抄）
  • There is no website — Google, Yelp and Visit Mississippi each show different opening hours
  • Your Facebook page is behind a login wall — people without an account see nothing
  • Ranked #1 of 52 restaurants in Corinth on Tripadvisor — and nowhere online says so

Town & Country Steak House保留

  • Your “About Us” link goes to the directory company’s page, not yours
  • The directory site’s footer carries their ads — on a page you pay for
  • The map link has the wrong GPS coordinates (latitude is negative)
