Since 1974

Fifty years, one building,
three people.

Abe, his wife Terri and their son Ryan. That is the whole staff, and it has been the whole staff for a very long time.

The grill has been at 803 Highway 72 West since 1974 — long enough to be called the oldest continuously operating diner on this stretch of Route 72. Opening at four in the morning is not a gimmick; it is what a highway town needs from a kitchen.
